Tom's Specialty

Feeding Champions

Athletic team catering is one of our greatest points of pride. Since the founding of Top This! Catering, we have had the honor of serving 19 minor league, collegiate, and high school championship teams across the Triangle area and beyond.

We understand the unique demands of team catering — timing, portion size, dietary variety, and the logistics of feeding a large group efficiently. We handle all of it so coaches and athletes can stay focused on the game.

What We Offer for Teams

  • Pre-game and post-game team meals
  • Bulk catering for rosters of any size
  • Flexible delivery and on-site service options
  • Menus designed around athletic nutrition
  • Custom options to accommodate dietary needs
  • Reliable scheduling around game and practice calendars

Our Story

Top This! Catering was founded in [YEAR FOUNDED] with a simple purpose: to bring great food and genuine hospitality to the Triangle area. Before Pastrami Tom's was ever an idea, Top This! was already building a reputation for quality and reliability — one event at a time.

What started as a local catering operation quickly grew into something bigger. Over the years, we've had the honor of fueling championship athletes, making wedding receptions unforgettable, and feeding the hardworking teams behind countless corporate events.

We are proud to have served 19 minor league, collegiate, and high school championship teams — and that number keeps growing. Whether it's a 10-person business lunch or a 300-guest wedding reception, our commitment never changes: fresh food, reliable service, and a personal touch.

Top This! Catering remains locally owned and operated, and we wouldn't have it any other way.
